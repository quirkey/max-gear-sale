Selling gear to get Max in the clear

Max's Surgery Gear Sale

A few years ago, Max came into our lives and quickly became an important member of our family. At > 25 lbs, and a purr louder than a jet engine, he has many names: Pooshkin, Plumpy, Big Boy.

This summer, a small cut appeared on his foot and strangely did not heal. After seeing multiple vets, the diagnosis was an aggressive form of cancer and the only treatment is to amputate his whole leg.

Of course, this is extremely expensive. With my current financial situation I don't feel right asking for donations, as there are much more deserving people and causes. However, can I interest you in some music gear? Some of these items I've built myself, some I'm just reselling. I figure I can feed two birds with one scone here and raise a few dollars to offset the cost of this surgery and clean out some of the things in my studio.

I'd love to avoid the fees of Reverb, so if you're interested in anything, DM me on Instagram (@quirkey) or email me at aaron@quirkey.com and we can arrange payment through Venmo or paypal.

If you're local to Kingston, I'm happy to set up pickup or delivery, too!
